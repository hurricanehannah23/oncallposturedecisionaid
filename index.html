How it's scored
same as the METCON threat matrix · worse of Kennedy and the Cape

    
    
      
  • Lightning: NWS probability of thunder: 10% Low, 25% Moderate, 55% High. An SPC thunder area raises it to at least Low, Marginal or higher to at least Moderate.
    • 
      
  • Surface winds: NWS forecast winds: Moderate for sustained 18 kt or gusts 25 kt, High for 35 kt (sustained or gusts), otherwise Low.
    • 
      
  • Winds aloft (≤400 ft): same grading, using the highest GFS sustained wind and estimated gust at 262, 328 or 394 ft (gust = sustained at that height plus the 33 ft gust excess).
    • 
      
  • Severe / hail: SPC Day 1–3 category: Marginal Low, Slight Moderate, Enhanced or higher High. Days 4–8 use the SPC 15% area as Moderate and 30% as High.
    • 
      
  • Heavy rain / flooding: NWS rainfall total (QPF) for the day: 0.25″ Low, 1″ Moderate, 2″ High.
    • 
      
  • Temperature: heat index 98° / 103° / 108°, or low temperature 45° / 38° / 32°.
    • 
      
  • Tropical: NWS chance of tropical-storm-force winds: 5% Low, 20% Moderate, 50% High (or hurricane-force 5% Moderate, 20% High).
    • 
      
  • Active NWS watches and advisories raise the matching row to Moderate on the days they cover, and warnings raise it to High. Marine and coastal products aren't scored.
    • 
      
  • Today only counts the hours left in the day.

Daily peaks
highest wind each ET day, with the time it happens (ET)

    

    
Forecasts from Open-Meteo. GFS and ECMWF have 33, 262, 328 and 394 ft (10, 80, 100, 120 m) out to 5 days; HRRR only publishes 33 and 262 ft (10, 80 m) and runs out about 48 h. Models only forecast gusts at 33 ft; in Gusts mode the higher levels are estimates (sustained wind at that height plus the 33 ft gust excess), so treat them as a guide. Colour breaks at 18, 25, 35 and 50 kt line up with the threat matrix wind criteria. The daily Winds aloft rating uses GFS at KSC and the Cape.
